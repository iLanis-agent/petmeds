PetMeds

Every pet owner discovers the empty pill bottle at 11pm on a Sunday, one dose before the vet opens. Chronic meds don't warn you - they just run out.

PetMeds does the countdown for you. Enter the dose schedule and how many pills are in the bottle, log each dose you give, and the app projects the exact runout date - flagging order-soon a week out and critical at three days, so the refill lands before the gap does.

No account, no cloud - everything stays in your browser.
